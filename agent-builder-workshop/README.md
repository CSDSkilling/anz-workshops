# Agent Workshop

Two sessions, one worksheet each. Download the worksheet, save a copy to **your OneDrive**, and fill it in as we go.

> **Before you start:** do not include sensitive information, Tax File Numbers, customer or passenger data, or confidential process details. Not sure? Ask your organisation's AI support team.

---

## Session 1 – What should become an agent?

### 📥 [Download the Session 1 worksheet](worksheets/Agent%20Workshop%20-%20Session%201%20Worksheet%20v1_0.docx)

*On the next screen, select the download button (↓) at the top right. Save it to your OneDrive as `Session 1 – [your name] – [idea].docx`.*

Then work through the steps in the worksheet:

1. **Which runway are you on?** Decide who is affected if it goes wrong, and confirm the two bright lines.
2. **Map the process today.** Write down the outcome, where the process starts and ends, the steps, and the evidence.
3. **Simplify before you automate.** What can be eliminated, simplified or standardised?
4. **Redesign with AI.** If AI were available from the start, how would you redesign the work?
5. **Prompt, agent or neither?** Make the call and say why.
6. **The four questions.** Check scenario, knowledge, value and risk, then write your one-line agent idea.

✅ Save your worksheet and bring it to Session 2. Paste your agent idea in the Teams chat.

---

## Session 2 – Build your agent

### 📥 [Download the Session 2 worksheet](worksheets/Agent%20Workshop%20-%20Session%202%20Worksheet%20v1_0.docx)

*Have your Session 1 worksheet open. Save this one to your OneDrive as `Session 2 – [your name] – [agent].docx`.*

Then work through the steps in the worksheet as you build in **Microsoft 365 Copilot Agent Builder**:

1. **Name it.** Give it a name, a one-line description, and your runway.
2. **Instruct it.** Fill in the instruction block and copy it into Agent Builder.
3. **Ground it.** Add your approved knowledge sources and three starter prompts.
4. **Test it.** Run a normal, an ambiguous and an out-of-scope request, and record what happened.
5. **Own it.** Write your success measure, and name the owner, the reviewer and who keeps it current. List the known limitations.

✅ Save your worksheet. It's your record of the agent's design, tests and ownership.

*You need a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence to build in Session 2. Without one, you can pair with someone who has a licence.*
